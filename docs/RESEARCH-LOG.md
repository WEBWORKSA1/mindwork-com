## Trademark check on the name

Search of US trademark listings on 19 November 2026 found, among others: MINDWORKS registered to Mindworks Inc.
(Santa Barbara, meditation training, class 41, reg. 5187660); MINDWORKS registered to Mindworks, Inc. (New Jersey,
classes 9 and 41, reg. 5265934); MINDWORKS applied for by Mindworks Technologies, Inc. (workflow software);
MINDWORKS LEARNING filed for cognitive-skills training software and workbooks; MINDWORK GROUP (new application).
Result: the site uses "MindWork.com" only as the name of the website at its domain, never the plural, never as a
brand for meditation training, courses or software, and carries a disclosure page. This is not legal advice.
