Disclaimer

What the tests, tools and guides on this site are, and what they are not.

Not medical or psychological advice

MindWork.com provides general information and self-help tools for healthy adults. Nothing on the site is medical, psychological or psychiatric advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not use it as a substitute for seeing a qualified professional. If you are concerned about your memory, attention, mood or sleep, talk to a doctor.

The tests are not diagnostic

The cognitive tests are simplified browser versions of research tasks. Results are affected by your device, your screen, your internet connection, the time of day and how you feel. They are not IQ tests, are not validated clinical instruments, and cannot detect or rule out any condition. The result labels and ranges are informal guides.

No promised results

We do not claim that using this site will improve intelligence, memory, job performance or health. Research on brain training is mixed, and we describe it honestly in our guide.

Breathing, sound and exercise

Stop the breathing pacer if you feel dizzy or unwell. Keep the noise mixer at a comfortable volume. Check with a doctor before changing your exercise or sleep routine if you have a health condition.

Accuracy

We work to keep the guides accurate and list our sources, but research changes and mistakes happen. Tell us through the contact page and we will correct them.

Advertising and affiliations

The site is funded by advertising, sponsors and supporters. Advertisers do not influence our content. An advertisement is not an endorsement.
