Three tests chosen for today, the same for every visitor. Do them in any order. Finishing all three extends your streak.

      Why a daily set

      Short, regular measurements tell you more than occasional long ones. Reaction time and attention scores move with sleep, stress and time of day, and a week of results shows your pattern.

      The workout is a gauge and a warm-up. It will not raise your intelligence; the brain-training guide explains what the evidence does and does not show.

      All nine tests

      • Reaction timeClick when the pad turns green. Five trials, averaged in milliseconds.
      • Number memoryRemember a number that grows by one digit each round.
      • Sequence memoryRepeat a growing pattern of tiles. One slip ends the run.
      • Visual memoryTiles flash on a grid. Click the same tiles from memory.
      • Stroop testName the ink colour, ignore the word. Measures interference in milliseconds.
      • 2-backPress Match when a letter repeats the one two steps back.
      • Mental mathSixty seconds of sums. The next one appears the instant you are right.
      • Verbal memorySeen it or new? Keep words apart as the list grows.
      • Schulte tableFind the numbers 1 to 25 in order, as fast as you can.
