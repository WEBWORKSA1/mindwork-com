How we write and correct our pages

Our standards for evidence, sources, advertising and corrections.

Evidence first

Guides and tool pages are built from published research. Each page lists the studies it relies on so you can check our reading of them. Where findings conflict or failed to replicate, we say so, even when a simpler story would be more appealing.

How pages are produced

Pages are drafted with the help of AI tools from published research and edited by the people who run the site. Drafting tools can make mistakes, which is one reason every page names its sources. If you find an error, tell us through the contact page and we will fix it and note the correction.

Claims we do not make

  • We do not say our tests measure intelligence or diagnose anything.
  • We do not say brain training makes you smarter in general.
  • We do not publish testimonials or user numbers we cannot verify.

Advertising and sponsorship

Advertising is served by Google AdSense and is kept visually separate from tests and tools. Sponsors may fund a tool, a guide or a contest and are named as sponsors on that page. Neither advertisers nor sponsors see or change editorial content before it is published.

The research radar and video shelf

These two pages are assembled automatically once a day from public feeds, filtered by topic. Appearing there is not an endorsement. We link to the original publisher and do not copy article text.

Corrections

We correct factual errors as soon as we confirm them. Significant corrections are noted at the foot of the page.
