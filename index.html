    Common questions

    Is everything free?

    Yes. Every test, tool, model and guide is free to use without an account. The site is funded by advertising, sponsors and supporters.

    Where are my scores stored?

    In your own browser, using local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, which also means your scores do not follow you to another device. You can export or delete them from the dashboard.

    Do these tests measure intelligence?

    No. Each one measures a narrow skill on a given day, on your device. They are useful for tracking your own state over time and are not IQ tests or medical assessments.

    Will the tests make me smarter?

    Practice makes you better at the test you practise. Evidence that this carries over to everyday thinking is weak, and we say so in the brain-training guide.
