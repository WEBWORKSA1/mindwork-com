Six tools for focused work

Each tool runs in this page, saves its state in your browser and needs no account. Pick the one that fits the problem in front of you.

  • Focus timerA Pomodoro timer with a task list and a daily count.
  • Breathing pacerGuided slow breathing in four patterns, from one to ten minutes.
  • Noise mixerBrown, pink and white noise, rain and a low hum, mixed in your browser.
  • Eisenhower matrixSort tasks by urgent and important, then act on each box differently.
  • Decision matrixScore options against weighted criteria and see which one leads.
  • Time-block plannerPaint your day in half-hour blocks of deep work, shallow work, meetings and breaks.

Which tool for which problem

If this is the problemUse this
You cannot get started, or you drift after ten minutesFocus timer
You feel wound up before a meeting or between tasksBreathing pacer
The room is noisy and voices keep pulling you awayNoise mixer
Everything feels urgentEisenhower matrix
You are stuck between optionsDecision matrix
The day disappears into meetings and messagesTime-block planner
