The free 7-day focus reset

One small change a day for a week, using the free tools on this site. About ten minutes a day. Day 1 is open below; sign up to unlock the rest in your browser.

What you get

  • A seven-day plan with one clear action a day
  • A before-and-after measurement of your own reaction time, attention and memory span
  • A printable version to keep on your desk
  • Occasional email when a new tool or guide is published

It is free, and it stays free. The site is paid for by advertising and supporters.

The plan

Day 1: Measure your baseline

Take the reaction time, Stroop and number memory tests at a time you can repeat next week. Write down the hour. You are measuring your starting point, not competing.

Sign up above to unlock days 2 to 7

Day 2: Protect one block

Tonight, choose one task for tomorrow and its first physical action. Block 60 minutes for it in the time-block planner, as early in the day as your calendar allows.

Day 3: Close the exits

For today’s block: email and chat fully closed, phone in another room. Run two 25-minute sessions on the focus timer. Note how many times you reached for something.

Day 4: Batch your messages

Check email and chat at three fixed times only, for example 10:00, 13:00 and 16:00. At the end of the day, write down what went wrong because of it. Usually nothing did.

Day 5: Sort the list

Put every open task into the Eisenhower matrix. Do one item from the top-left box, put dates on the top-right box, and drop or hand off two things from the bottom row.

Day 6: Recover properly

Take a ten-minute walk between blocks instead of opening a feed. Do three minutes of long-exhale breathing on the breathing pacer before your hardest task. Stop caffeine by early afternoon and dim the lights an hour before bed.

Day 7: Measure again and plan the week

Retake the three tests at the same hour as Day 1 and compare on your dashboard. Then block next week’s focus sessions before the week fills up. Keep the two changes that helped most.

Questions

Do I have to sign up?

No. Day 1 is open, and every tool the plan uses is free without an account. Signing up unlocks the full seven days on this page and adds you to the update list.

What will you email me?

Occasional notes about new tools and guides. Your address is not sold or shared, and every email will tell you how to stop them.

Will a week change anything?

A week is long enough to find out which one or two changes help you most. It is not long enough to build a habit; that takes keeping those two changes going.

Is this a medical or therapy programme?

No. It is a set of work habits. If you are struggling with attention, sleep or mood in a way that affects your life, talk to a doctor.
