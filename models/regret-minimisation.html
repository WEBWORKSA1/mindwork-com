Regret minimisation

Choose what your older self would be least sorry about.

What it is

For large personal decisions, project yourself decades ahead and ask which choice you would regret more. The exercise strips out short-term discomfort, which dominates in the moment and barely registers in memory.

How to use it

  1. Picture yourself at 80, looking back.
  2. Ask which you would regret more: having tried this and failed, or never having tried.
  3. Write the answer and the reason in one sentence.
  4. Check it against your obligations to other people before acting.

At work

An engineer weighing a move into management pictures both paths at 80. She would not regret trying and returning to engineering; she would regret never finding out.

Where it goes wrong

It favours action over caution by design. For decisions with irreversible downsides, pair it with a sober look at the worst case.

Where it comes from

Jeff Bezos has described using this framing when he left a finance job to start Amazon.

Use it today: write one decision you are facing this week and run it through the steps above. Two lines is enough.
