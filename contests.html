Rules

  • Free to enter. No purchase or payment improves your chances.
  • Open to people aged 18 or older, where such contests are lawful. Void where prohibited.
  • One entry per person per round. Scores must be your own, made on this site during the round, without automation.
  • Entries are ranked by score. Ties go to the earlier entry. We may ask for a screen recording before confirming a result, and may refuse entries we cannot verify.
