About MindWork.com

A free mental gym and thinking toolkit for people who think for a living.

What this site is

Mind work is the kind of work most of us now do: reading, deciding, writing, designing, analysing, explaining. It depends on attention and memory far more than on time at a desk, and almost nobody is taught how to look after either.

MindWork.com collects the useful parts in one place. There are tests that show how sharp you are today, tools that make focused work easier to start, mental models for decisions and problems, and guides that explain the research in plain language.

What we promise

  • Free and open. No account, no paywall, no download.
  • Private by design. Your scores and plans stay in your browser.
  • Honest about evidence. We list sources, and we say when a popular claim does not hold up. We do not claim that our tests raise intelligence or treat any condition.
  • Clear about money. The site is funded by advertising, sponsors and voluntary support. Sponsored content is labelled.

How it is built

The site is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, generated from plain content files and hosted on GitHub Pages. A scheduled job refreshes the research radar and video shelf every day. The source lives in a public repository.

Who runs it

MindWork.com is an independent website operated from its own domain. It is not affiliated with any company or product with a similar name; see the trademark and copyright page.
